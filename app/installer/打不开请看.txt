Blueprint 打不开怎么办

Blueprint 现在还没有苹果的开发者签名，第一次打开时 macOS 会拦一下。按下面的顺序试，多数情况做到第 2 步就能打开。

1. 先把 Blueprint 拖进「应用程序」文件夹，再到「应用程序」里双击打开它。
   如果弹出「Apple 无法验证“Blueprint”……」，点「完成」。

2. 打开「系统设置」→「隐私与安全性」，往下翻到「安全性」，
   能看到「已阻止“Blueprint”以保护你的 Mac」，点旁边的「仍要打开」，
   按提示输入开机密码，再点一次「仍要打开」。
   「仍要打开」只在第 1 步之后一小时左右出现；没看到的话，回到第 1 步再双击一次。

3. 如果提示的是「“Blueprint”已损坏，无法打开」：
   (1) 按 ⌘ + 空格，输入「终端」，按回车，打开终端。
   (2) 复制下面这一行，粘贴进终端，按回车：

       xattr -cr /Applications/Blueprint.app

   (3) 没有任何输出就是成功了。回到「应用程序」再双击 Blueprint。
   如果弹出「“终端”想要管理 Mac 上的 App」，点「允许」，再运行一次这行。
   如果显示 No such file，说明 Blueprint 还没拖进「应用程序」，先做第 1 步的拖动。

这行命令只去掉 macOS 给「从网上下载的文件」加的标记，不会改动 Blueprint 本身。
以后下载新版本替换时，可能需要再做一次。


If Blueprint won't open

Blueprint is not signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks it on first launch. Try these in order; step 2 is usually enough.

1. Drag Blueprint into the Applications folder, then double-click it there.
   If a message says Apple could not verify "Blueprint", click Done.

2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
   Next to "Blueprint" was blocked to protect your Mac, click Open Anyway,
   enter your login password, then click Open Anyway again.
   The button only shows for about an hour after step 1. If you don't see it, double-click Blueprint again.

3. If the message says "Blueprint" is damaged and can't be opened:
   (1) Press Command + Space, type Terminal, press Return.
   (2) Paste this line into Terminal and press Return:

       xattr -cr /Applications/Blueprint.app

   (3) No output means it worked. Open Blueprint from Applications again.
   If macOS asks whether Terminal may manage apps on your Mac, click Allow and run the line again.
   "No such file" means Blueprint is not in Applications yet; drag it there first.

The command only removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag macOS adds. It does not change Blueprint itself.
You may need to do this again after downloading a new version.
